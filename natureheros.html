Marine Conservation

Afroz Shah

Mumbai, Maharashtra

Afroz Shah is an Indian environmental activist and lawyer from Mumbai.He is best known for organizing the world's largest beach clean-up. In 2016, Shah was awarded the United Nations Champion of the Earth title for singlehandedly initiating & leading the clean-up of Mumbai's Versova Beach.

Ripu Daman Bevli

New Delhi, India

Ripu Daman Bevli is an environmentalist, and social activist.He has been referred as the Plogman of India for his contributions to the littering crisis in India and for introducing the concept of plogging to India. He is one of the ambassadors of FIT India Movement.

Sunita Narain

New Delhi, India

Sunita Narain is a prominent environmentalist and the Director General of the Centre for Science and Environment (CSE) in Delhi. She focuses on key civic and environmental issues such as air pollution, water conservation, urban sanitation, solid waste management, and climate change advocacy.
